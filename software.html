About the project

The app grew up

The old page showed the French pronunciation prototype. The current app is broader: French, English, and Chinese, with speaking, vocabulary, and guided review.

Learning support

What it helps with

Pronunciation practice

Record, hear yourself, compare, and try again. The microphone is patient. Very patient.

AI-assisted feedback

Clear pronunciation signals for sounds and words, without pretending a score is a human teacher.

Vocabulary cards and maps

Swipe cards, save favorites, follow learning paths, and avoid the classic "open app, forget plan" situation.
