Academic Path

An academic journey across France, the United Kingdom, and China.

I usually go by Sifaks, or Danny in English — and 邬熙汐 in Chinese. Take your pick. I like languages, and I like figuring out how we learn them even more. That’s probably how I reached a near-native level in Chinese (Mandarin — not every dialect, not yet). When I’m not studying languages, I build things: my other passion is sharing how to learn efficiently, and I hope you find something useful here.

Fun fact: I can do a backflip on ice, and I once hit Challenger in League of Legends. Each happened only once.
